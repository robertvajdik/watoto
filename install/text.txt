WHO WE ARE
*Malezi na Watoto* is a community-based organization committed to improving the well-being, development, and future of young children and their families.
We work closely with parents, caregivers, and communities to promote positive parenting, early childhood development, and supportive environments where every child can learn, grow, and thrive.
Our work focuses particularly on children in their early years, supporting parents and caregivers with knowledge, practical skills, and opportunities that strengthen children’s development and family well-being.
Through community engagement, parenting sessions, Dialogic Booksharing, learning activities, and collaboration with local stakeholders, we create spaces where children are encouraged to explore, communicate, learn, and develop with confidence.
 Our Vision
A community where every child receives the care, protection, stimulation, and opportunities they need to reach their full potential.
Our Mission
To empower parents, caregivers, and communities with knowledge and practical opportunities that promote positive parenting, early childhood development, and the well-being of children.
What We Do
* Promote positive parenting and responsive caregiving.
* Support early childhood development and learning.
* Conduct Dialogic Booksharing and child-centered learning activities.
* Empower parents and caregivers with practical parenting knowledge.
* Mobilize communities to support children’s well-being.
* Collaborate with local stakeholders to create sustainable solutions for children and families.
